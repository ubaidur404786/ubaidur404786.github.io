Machine Learning Engineer Intern · Edge AI / TinyML

CRIStAL Laboratory (FOX Team) & Inria Lille
Apr 2026 – Sep 2026
  • Problem: most time-series classifiers are black boxes. They give a label but not the part of the signal that led to it, which makes them hard to trust in health or industrial use.
  • Started from MILLET, which gives both a prediction and a per-timestep explanation. It works well, but the model is too large to run on tiny devices.
  • Designed SEA-NET (separable TCN blocks + Top-k Conjunction Pooling) that keeps the built-in explanations with only ~41K parameters.
  • Results: ~10× faster inference and accuracy up from ~89% to ~94%, small enough for a microcontroller, so the model can be used day to day and still show why it made each decision.
  • Handled the whole cycle: architecture, tracked experiments, evaluation and compression. Paper under review at a major ML conference.

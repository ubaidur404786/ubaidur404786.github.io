Université Côte d'Azur

Student Researcher, Deep Learning

Nov 2025 – Apr 2026
  • LC-MS bacteria recognition with CHU Laval: PCA + AE / VAE / CAE encoders with an MLP, 87% vs 82% for classical ML on 28 bacteria.
  • Each sample is a ~100K-value spectrum with a strong batch effect across processing days.

Can a urine sample tell you which of 28 bacteria is causing an infection in a pregnant woman? Classical ML had reached 82% on this data. Our deep learning pipeline reached 87%.

The data fought back: each sample is about 100,000 values, and samples processed on different days carried a strong batch effect. Most of the work went into the data: feature engineering and PCA, then compressing samples with an Autoencoder, a VAE and a CAE. The best encoder plus an MLP classifier gave the 87%.

Supervised by Frédéric Precioso, in collaboration with Prof. Arnaud Droit's team at CHU Laval (Canada).
